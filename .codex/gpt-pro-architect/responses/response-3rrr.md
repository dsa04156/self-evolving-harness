The separate scorer records an oracle-access event after the prediction seal, requires exact occurrence multiplicity, and emits a signed score report. The chronology, prediction commitment, oracle-access event, and score report reproduce byte-for-byte under the independent verifier.

The reported top-1, top-3, and top-8 counts are accepted only as visible-fixture development diagnostics. Because the heuristic was designed with knowledge of the public semantic categories, these values are not independent evaluation and cannot become attribution-gate evidence through more enthusiastic wording.
