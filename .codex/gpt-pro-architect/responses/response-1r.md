DECISION: REVISE

NOVELTY:
The novelty boundary is now acceptable for a bounded prototype. The packet correctly limits the contribution to a standalone runtime plus typed, content-addressed composition, lifecycle separation, principal enforcement, and evidence-linked whole-harness promotion. It no longer pretends that trace analysis, worktrees, bounded proposals, held-out evaluation, or the broad incantation “self-improvement” sprang fully formed from this project. There is no remaining novelty blocker at Gate 1R, although publication-grade novelty still depends on the referenced source ledger and later implementation evidence.

CORRECTNESS:
The immutable-manifest/external-record split, authoritative type registry, expanded behavior closure, declarative mutation languages, whole-composition evaluation, and runtime-state snapshots substantially close the prior correctness findings. Two state-machine defects remain implementation-blocking. First, the global harness lifecycle still conflates qualification with deployment: `active`, `rolled_back`, and `retired` cannot be reconciled cleanly with channel-scoped deployment pointers, retained rollback targets, or multiple channels. Second, the session lifecycle has no abnormal terminal path for initialization failure, cancellation, budget exhaustion, security termination, unrecoverable blockage, or host-enforced shutdown. Gate 2 cannot implement those semantics without quietly making up a new contract, the traditional human method of converting ambiguity into bugs.

EVALUATION FAIRNESS:
The mandatory B5-SM and B6-ABL controls, separate `K_A`/`K_B`, complete provider accounting, expiring unused resources, and separation of evolution versus final inference cost are materially better. H1a and H1b now support defensible causal wording within their stated limits. H3 is still not executable from this packet because no named raw-trace counterfactual arm appears in the revised baseline matrix, despite H3 continuing to claim an evidence-representation efficiency comparison. The gate-result release policy also prevents within-batch adaptation but does not prevent cross-run or cross-protocol adaptation after the aggregate packet is released. Finally, the higher-cost promotion exception refers to a “smoothed success-per-token gain” without defining the formula that promotion machinery must apply.

GENERALIZATION:
The distinction between a withheld public Terminal-Bench test and genuinely sealed data is correct. The temporal-holdout governance is appropriately conservative, and the minimum-task fallback is disclosed rather than disguised as adequate power. The remaining generalization risk is reuse of `D_gate`: a post-selection aggregate can still influence later protocol revisions, method choices, or reruns unless its recipients, release time, and single-use consequences are frozen. H4 is absent from the revised hypothesis section; the contract should state explicitly whether cross-model transfer has been withdrawn or remains a stretch hypothesis.

REPRODUCIBILITY:
The canonicalization profile, transitive closure accounting, snapshot pinning, protocol-scoped immutability, phase ledger, and task-clustered statistics form a credible reproducibility target. The listed hashes establish artifact identity, not the correctness of artifact contents, so Gate 2 evidence must include the actual hash-matched contract bundle and validator outputs. One packet-level fixture inconsistency remains: fourteen multi-cause pairs over seven families can form a degree-four graph overall, but not “degree four per family per stratum” across multiple strata unless there are fourteen pairs in every stratum. The fixture builder must not be allowed to resolve that arithmetic by artistic interpretation.

SECURITY:
The principal matrix, provider proxy, authenticated Unix-socket framing, peer-credential checks, signatures, fail-closed behavior, fresh containers, scoped mounts, denied network paths, and explicit TCB assumptions are sufficient as a target architecture. Language separation is correctly demoted from security mechanism to engineering choice. The missing abnormal session-termination path is also a security blocker: an unauthorized action or surviving child process must cause a modeled, auditable terminal transition with authority revocation, not merely an out-of-band container kill that the evidence model cannot represent.

CLAIM DISCIPLINE:
The claim lock is strong. Gate 1R does not claim runtime correctness, actual isolation, benchmark improvement, autonomous improvement, model reproducibility, or production security. Negative results and unresolved empirical fields remain visible. Keep that posture. The packet may support architecture implementation only after the remaining contract ambiguities are removed.

BLOCKING FINDINGS:

1. The harness lifecycle conflates artifact qualification with channel deployment. The meanings of `active`, `rolled_back`, and `retired` are incompatible with channel-scoped pointers, retained rollback targets, and possible multi-channel deployment.
2. The session lifecycle has no terminal abnormal path from `created`, `initialized`, `running`, `waiting`, `blocked`, or `recovering`. Cancellation, budget exhaustion, security termination, initialization failure, and irrecoverable recovery therefore have no legal state transition.
3. H3 has no explicit raw-trace comparison arm in the revised executable baseline list. Its cost-ratio estimand consequently has no unambiguous numerator, denominator, candidate-selection procedure, or matched treatment contrast.
4. The gate policy blocks adaptation inside one candidate batch but does not define who receives the post-selection aggregate, when they receive it, or whether the same `D_gate` may remain confirmatory after that information influences later protocol or method decisions.
5. The promotion exception requires a 5% “smoothed success-per-token gain,” but the smoothing constants, aggregation level, exact formula, and handling of zero usage, missing usage, caps, and timeouts are undefined.
6. The HarnessFaultBench multi-cause balance statement is internally inconsistent unless there is only one stratum. Fourteen edges give degree four per family overall for seven families, not degree four per family in each of multiple strata.

REQUIRED REVISIONS:

1. Separate harness qualification from deployment. Replace global `active` with an unambiguous state such as `approved` or `deployable`, and make channel deployment exist only through `DeploymentPointerRecord`. State whether protocol v1 permits one channel or multiple channels. Replacing a channel pointer must not retire the prior target. Rollback must be a CAS pointer event to an approved, protocol-compatible retained target. A version may be retired only when it is not referenced by any channel, pinned session or descendant, rollback target, pending evaluation, or required audit record.
2. Remove `rolled_back` as a global terminal artifact state, or define it only as a deployment-event annotation scoped to a specific channel generation. A candidate rolled back on one channel must not become globally unusable if the protocol permits another channel to reference it.
3. Add a modeled abnormal-termination path to the session state machine. One acceptable contract is `any nonterminal state → terminating → terminated`, with a required reason enum covering at least initialization failure, user cancellation, budget exhaustion, deadline expiry, verifier failure, security violation, process crash, and unrecoverable recovery. Termination must revoke descendant leases and capabilities, stop backend jobs, seal final evidence and accounting, and prohibit resume; any retry must create a new session.
4. Add an explicit H3 control arm, for example `B6-RAW`, identical to the layered-evidence treatment in model, source events, candidate count, seeds, mutation grammar, attribution workflow, solver work, gate handling, and budgets, differing only in evidence representation. Freeze the cost-ratio direction, quality endpoints, splits, candidate-selection rule, and failure handling. If no such isolated treatment is feasible, withdraw H3 rather than estimating it through unrelated baselines.
5. Freeze gate information governance across the entire protocol lifecycle. All method arms, selection rules, and report templates must be content-addressed before first `D_gate` access. Gate task results and the aggregate selection packet may be read only by the fixed selector/promoter and audit authority until all confirmatory final evaluations are complete. Any release to proposers, developers, protocol authors, or model-selection personnel makes that gate exploratory for subsequent work; later confirmatory runs must use a fresh gate split.
6. State whether gate use is one-shot per protocol or reusable under a predeclared non-adaptive replication rule. Record every access, recipient, released field, and release time. “Six feedback events” must distinguish feedback consumed by the method from evidence retained only for audit.
7. Define the higher-cost exception as an exact deterministic formula. The contract must specify the success numerator, token denominator, smoothing constants and units, task aggregation, treatment of failed and timed-out tasks, missing provider usage, reserved-cap charging, and the exact inequality used for the 5% threshold. The validator and promoter must compute the same value from the signed ledger without discretionary interpretation.
8. Correct the HarnessFaultBench multi-cause specification. If fourteen pairs are total, state that the seven-family graph has degree four per family overall and enumerate each edge plus its assigned difficulty stratum. If degree four is required independently within each stratum, increase and freeze the total pair count accordingly. Split counts, fixture IDs, and the split-manifest hash must be updated together if the count changes.
9. Explicitly dispose of H4. State either that cross-model transfer is withdrawn from protocol v1, or restore it with the second-model selection, freeze timing, no-retuning rule, evaluation split, budget treatment, and authorized claim.
10. Resubmit only the changed lifecycle schema, deployment/rollback invariants, session transition table, H3 comparison row, gate-feedback policy, promotion-cost formula, corrected fixture graph, and resulting replacement hashes. Do not bury these corrections under another festival of architectural diagrams.

NON-BLOCKING RECOMMENDATIONS:

1. Define `retired` as “ineligible for new sessions or deployments,” not deletion. Content, evidence, and rollback-relevant artifacts should remain retained according to an explicit reference policy.
2. Predeclare H1 handling for invalid candidates, no-op candidates, zero parent regressions, and undefined relative-reduction denominators.
3. Pin the exact Node.js, Python, container-base, dependency-lock, canonicalizer, and validator digests before Gate 2 evidence is accepted; `24.x` and `3.13` are development families, not reproducible identities.
4. Include the hash-matched machine-readable contract bundle in Gate 2 evidence. Raw SHA-256 values are useful, but hashes cannot testify about the wisdom of the bytes they identify.

AUTHORIZED NEXT SCOPE:
Only the named research and contract corrections are authorized: harness qualification/deployment separation, rollback and retirement invariants, abnormal session termination, the H3 raw-trace control, protocol-lifetime gate governance, the deterministic cost-exception formula, the corrected multi-cause fixture balance, and explicit H4 disposition. No runtime-kernel implementation, process-isolation implementation, evaluator implementation, benchmark fixture execution, provider call, gate access, promotion machinery, or rollback machinery is authorized until those revised contracts receive Gate 1 approval.
